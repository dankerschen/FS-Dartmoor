## Game, name and artwork

Forest Shuffle, Forest Shuffle: Dartmoor and Forest Shuffle: Smoky Mountains
are designed by Kosch, illustrated by Toni Llobet and Judit Piella, and
published by Lookout Games (Lookout GmbH). This calculator is independent and
unofficial, is not affiliated with or endorsed by the publisher, and contains
no official game artwork.

## Illustrations

The habitat banners, top-bar strip and section decorations are AI-generated
watercolour-and-ink illustrations made for this calculator with Nano Banana 2
(Google), from image prompts written with Claude (Anthropic), then cropped and
compressed for the web. They are new
compositions, not copies of any card, board or official illustration. Check Google's
terms for Nano Banana 2 output before reusing these files elsewhere.

## AI assistance

Developed with the help of AI tools, notably Perplexity and Claude
(Anthropic). The original category icons were drawn with Perplexity; the
image prompts and a few additional icons were made with Claude. AI-assisted work can contain mistakes: please check scores against
the official rulebook and appendix.

## Translations and terminology

French and German interface texts were written for this page with Claude
(Anthropic). Terminology sources:

- German card names: Lookout's official German appendices (Mischwald, Dartmoor,
  Smoky Mountains), https://www.lookout-spiele.de
- French section headings and card names for the base game and the Alpine
  expansion: the French rulebook of Forêt Mixte (https://regle.escaleajeux.fr/fomix_rg.pdf,
  https://ammd.ch/pdfregles/foretmixte.pdf) and a French score sheet for the
  Alpine expansion supplied by the author of this page.
- Dartmoor and Exmoor: Mikko Saari's MIT-licensed Dartmoor tool (French and German
  tables) and Lookout's French play aid for Dartmoor/Exmoor. Only names and
  short terms were taken from it; no rules text is reproduced.
- Lookout publishes no French appendix for the base game's other expansions or for
  Smoky Mountains, so their French card names are common French species names and
  may differ from the printed cards. A few promo names (Dartmoor DP01-DP03) are
  the author's own wording.

Official rulebooks and appendices are linked from the page footer
(lookout-spiele.de); they are not redistributed here.
