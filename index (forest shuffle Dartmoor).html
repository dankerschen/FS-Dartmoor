Scoring notes

Sessile Oak still counts trees and shrubs, following its card entry and example. Some publisher shrub descriptions conflict with this; check your printed card. This rule was not silently changed.

Habitat doubling is included in the plant or bird score, not as points for Blanket Bog or High Coastal Heath themselves.

Names not verified in the available publisher material stay in English. Promo formulas are retained; this is not independent verification of every promo rule.

Mark “Nobody has more moors” for every player tied for the greatest moor count, including universal moors.
